## Reverification principles applied

1. Current official company pages were preferred over historical notes.
2. Team size, internal systems, or operational pain were not inferred when the company did not publish enough evidence.
3. A public contact route does not automatically mean a prospect is approved for outbound.
4. Mature operators with their own technology remain valuable as benchmark interviews even when adoption probability appears lower.
5. Solo/small operators are intentionally deprioritized when their current structure does not match the locked ICP.
6. No company was marked as a buyer; all classifications remain prospecting hypotheses until first-party conversations occur.
