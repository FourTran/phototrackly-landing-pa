## Historical-data rule

Imported September material may contain stale company facts, dates, platform guidance, or old PhotoTrackly URLs. Reverify before external use. In particular, no `qrtrackly.com` PhotoTrackly URL should be published or sent.

## P1 execution boundaries

The consolidated material is preparation, not evidence that marketing happened. A prospect is not contacted until an actual outbound action is recorded in the CRM Activity tab. An interview is not completed until a real conversation is recorded in Interviews. A draft social item is not published until its actual public URL and publication date are recorded.
