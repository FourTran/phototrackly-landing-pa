# PhotoTrackly Founding Operator Offer v1

**Locked:** 2026-10-07  
**Markets:** United States and Australia  
**ICP:** see `ICP_V1.md`  
**Status:** approved non-commercial offer for P1 outreach and research. Commercial incentives remain unapproved.

## Offer name

**Founding Operator Workflow Review**

## One-sentence offer

For growing property-media teams, PhotoTrackly will review one real booking-to-delivery workflow with the operator, return a concise workflow-friction map they can use with their current tools, and—if there is strong fit—consider the team for a future founding pilot and invite them to help shape workflow priorities.

## Why this offer

Established competitors already make demos and onboarding easy. A generic “free demo” is therefore not enough differentiation for a pre-launch product.

PhotoTrackly should lead with something useful before asking for adoption:

1. understand one real job;
2. give the operator a useful operational artifact;
3. learn from the workflow;
4. optionally show the product direction;
5. only then discuss whether a future pilot makes sense.

This keeps the first interaction research-led rather than sales-led.

## What the operator gives us

During a 20-minute conversation, walk through one real property-media job:

- how the order arrived;
- who scheduled/assigned the shoot;
- who captured which services;
- where files landed;
- how an editor knew work was ready;
- how QC/revisions were handled;
- how delivery readiness was decided;
- which tools were used;
- where somebody had to check, chase, reconcile, or communicate status.

The operator does not need to prepare a deck or expose client-sensitive media.

## What PhotoTrackly gives back

After the conversation, we provide a short written **Workflow Friction Map** containing:

- the observed booking-to-delivery stages;
- key roles/owners;
- important handoffs;
- current systems/tools mentioned;
- visible status gaps or manual-check points;
- unclear ownership/exception points;
- one practical workflow improvement or checklist idea that can be used with the team’s current tools.

This deliverable must be grounded only in what the operator actually shared. Do not invent process facts or quantify savings.

## Optional concept walkthrough

If relevant, the final few minutes can show the current PhotoTrackly product direction.

Rules:

- workflow discussion comes first;
- a demo is optional;
- clearly label planned/in-development functionality;
- do not imply integrations/features are live unless they are;
- do not turn the review into a hidden sales call.

## Founding-pilot path

When a team strongly matches the ICP and the problem appears frequent/material:

- mark the prospect as a **Founding Pilot Candidate** in the private CRM;
- invite them to continue sharing feedback;
- give them priority consideration when an appropriate pilot is actually ready;
- use their workflow evidence to influence release priorities when it is representative and technically/business-feasible.

**Priority consideration is not guaranteed access.**

Do not promise any of these unless explicitly approved later:

- permanent discount;
- founding-user price;
- free months;
- guaranteed pilot access;
- launch date;
- guaranteed migration/onboarding;
- custom development;

- SLA/support level;

If a prospect asks about price, use:

> We haven’t locked founding-pilot pricing yet. Right now I’m focused on understanding whether the workflow problem is important enough to solve and what the first usable scope should include.

## Who gets the offer

Use primarily for **A-priority ICP prospects** and selected B prospects where a workflow review has high learning value.

Do not use it as a mass lead magnet.

Ideal recipient:

- founder/owner;
- operations manager;
- production/studio manager;
- person responsible for job coordination and delivery visibility.

Short version:

> Open to a 20-minute workflow review? I’ll map the handoffs/status gaps from one real job and send it back to you afterward.

## Positioning statement

Use:

> PhotoTrackly is being built for growing property-media teams that need a clearer operational layer between booking, shoot, editing, QC, and delivery.

Avoid:

and:

> PhotoTrackly is an AI operating system that automates everything.

## Success criteria for the offer

Do not judge this offer mainly by website registrations.

Track:

- outreach sent;
- replies;
- accepted workflow reviews;
- held reviews;
- Workflow Friction Maps completed;
- repeated pain themes;
- explicit “not a problem” responses;
- teams qualifying as Founding Pilot Candidates;
- referrals to another operator.

After the first 5 held reviews, revisit:

- Did the artifact feel useful to operators?
- Did the offer make it easier to get conversations?
- Did the same operational problems repeat?
- Did teams ask to see/test PhotoTrackly without being pushed?
- What promise, if any, would make a future pilot more compelling?
