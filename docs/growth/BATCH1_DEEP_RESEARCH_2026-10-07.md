## Cross-cohort learning targets

Use the same core questions across the five reviews:

1. Where is the actual job source of truth?
2. What signal means a handoff is ready?
3. Who notices a missing deliverable?
4. Who owns reschedules or client-detail changes after booking?
5. How are revisions represented?
6. What does Ops still need to ask another person to know?
7. Which existing system would they refuse to replace?
8. Is the operational pain frequent/material enough to justify another workflow layer?
