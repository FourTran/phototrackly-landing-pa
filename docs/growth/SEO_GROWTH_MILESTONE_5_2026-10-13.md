## 5.4 — Reproducible AI visibility baseline

The first-party fixture `docs/growth/geo-visibility-prompts-v1.json` contains:
- 12 **fixed** buyer prompts, split US/Australia, with target *existing* PhotoTrackly owner URLs.
- Four target experiences: ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini.
- A clear response-observation schema: observed time, platform, country, whether PhotoTrackly was named, whether a **clickable canonical citation** appeared, citation URLs, legacy-domain citations, fact accuracy, screenshot/export location, tester.
- States `NOT_TESTED`, `TESTED_WITH_CITATION`, `TESTED_WITHOUT_CITATION`, `TEST_UNAVAILABLE`.

**Important: The initial cross-platform run has NOT been executed.** This tool session does not have a connected, evidence-producing UI/API session for all four independent answer engines. Do not invent ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity citations or present "zero" as the outcome of 48 unperformed prompt executions. The benchmark framework is built and validated; platform-answer observations remain outstanding until an authorized tester executes it. Google Search Console does not provide general ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity citation counts.

Observed on October 13 via **GSC MCP v2 only**:
- Finalized Sep 14–Oct 11: **29 impressions, zero clicks**, no commercial-page impression rows.
- Query-shape classifier: **no classified branded queries** and no conventional conversational question cluster. One "comparison" candidate and multiple long copied/pasted-question patterns are low-fit; **heuristics are not a test for Google AI Overview or assistant visibility**.
- GA4 Oct 6–12: **1 Tag Assistant referral session**, zero key events. Sep 15–Oct 12: **523 sessions**, **521 Direct** with low engagement and 1 Tag Assistant referral. No identified organic or named AI-referral source; Direct traffic cannot be attributed to bots or customers with certainty. No verified qualified lead is shown by these metrics.

Observed independently in normal public web search: results still surfaced legacy `qrtrackly.com` PhotoTrackly content; because this is not an output from the named answer engines, it is a **brand-discovery risk**, not a ChatGPT citation baseline.

### How to conduct a valid repeated test

1. Execute the 12 prompts under the same market and date in each of the four actual AI/search interfaces with a documented logged-in mode and country. Capture exact answer and clickable cited URLs; do not count unsupported text mentions as citations.
2. Distinguish `https://phototrackly.com` from `https://qrtrackly.com`; mark old-domain citations separately.
3. Record *not tested / unable* when a provider is inaccessible or refuses to show an AI answer, rather than silently recording zero.
4. Repeat after independently verified production deployment and recrawl. Calculate citation rate only over **executed** prompt-platform pairs, never over all 48 if any were not tested.
5. Compare actual human acquisition and consenting qualified signups in GA4 and the verified registration ledger separately; do not invent keyword-to-registration attribution.
