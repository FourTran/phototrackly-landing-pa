## P1 goal

Create qualified discovery and customer conversations with operators of growing property-media businesses. Website visits are useful only when they lead to learning, replies, early-access registrations, pilot interest or other evidence of buyer intent.

## ICP v1

Prioritize founder-led or small-to-mid-sized real-estate photography / property-media companies that show at least two of these signals:

- multiple photographers, editors or production roles;
- multiple services on the same property job: photography, video, drone, floor plan, 3D, staging or similar;
- a founder, operations manager, studio manager or production role still coordinating status manually;
- job state moves across booking, assignment, shoot, file handoff, editing, QC/revision and delivery;
- multiple systems or portals are involved;
- next-day or similarly tight turnaround makes exceptions costly;
- the company already has demand and the operational problem is more important than finding its first clients.

### Deprioritize

- solo photographers whose main problem is lead generation;
- businesses where current public evidence indicates one person handles every shoot;
- companies with insufficient evidence that operational coordination is a recurring problem;
- mature software/platform operators when the primary value is benchmark research rather than near-term adoption.

## Founding Operator Research offer

Working label: **PhotoTrackly Founding Operator Research Group**.

The first ask is a short workflow conversation, not a sales demo.

Proposed public/private message:

> PhotoTrackly is being built for property-media teams where one job can pass through scheduling, photographers, editors, QC and delivery. We are speaking with operators who already manage that complexity. Show us how one real booking moves through your business, where status becomes unclear, and what you still have to chase manually. We will use those workflows to shape the first release and give strong-fit teams priority when the private pilot opens.

Guardrails:

- no promise of free lifetime use, discounts, equity, guaranteed pilot access or launch date;
- no claim that PhotoTrackly already solves the problem;
- no quantified savings or performance promises;
- customer interviews precede feature pitching;
- use real recent-event questions and preserve objections/counterexamples.

## Acquisition sequence

1. Reverify the 20 seed prospects.
2. Select five highest-learning-value prospects.
3. Research each company and contact route deeply.
4. Prepare one personalized message per prospect.
5. User reviews/approves Batch 1 before any outbound send.
6. Send one by one using verified public business routes.
7. Record every send/reply/opt-out/meeting in the CRM Activity tab.
8. Run 20-minute workflow interviews with responders.
9. Move repeated evidence into Feedback and Interviews.
10. Update homepage/offer/content language only from validated patterns.
11. Expand the list from 20 to 50 and then 100 only after Batch 1 learning.
12. Use community and founder-led distribution in parallel, without disguised promotion.

## Content policy for P1

The previous fixed-volume content strategy is retired. The scheduled job is now **PhotoTrackly Growth Content**.

New rules:

- prefer improving/distributing existing content when another URL would add overlap;
- use GSC MCP only for Google Search Console;
- use real customer/prospect/community language when available;
- create founder LinkedIn/community/outreach derivatives from existing content;

## Success measures

Early-stage P1 scorecard:

- researched prospects reverified;
- personalized first contacts sent;
- reply rate and reply quality;
- workflow conversations booked/held;
- repeated pain language;
- objections and existing tools;
- early-access/founding-pilot interest;
- referral/community traffic;
- qualified direct traffic;
- first Google impressions/indexing movement as a supporting channel, not the only acquisition channel.

Do not treat page count, raw impressions, automated crawler traffic or unqualified visits as customer validation.
