# Resource editorial policy

These four guides are practical operating templates, not customer case studies or claims about released PhotoTrackly features. They are written for US and Australian property media teams. Before publishing further guides:

- Use real workflow questions from consented discovery conversations when possible. Remove identifying property and client details.
- Keep examples labeled as examples; do not invent survey results, customer quotes or measured savings.
- Review each guide for accurate scope and a useful next action. Update an existing guide when a question fits rather than generating near-duplicate pages.
- Link each guide to the relevant planned product page and to the early-access form. Record substantive updates with a real date.
- Inspect impressions, useful search queries, and confirmed lead registrations monthly. Bing AI citations can supplement this review; they do not guarantee leads or coverage.

### Review checklist
- Who: the accountable publisher is PhotoTrackly editorial. There is no invented named author, client, reviewer or professional qualification.
- How: an illustrative example written from the planned job model and explicitly disclosed as fictional; AI assistance may be used in drafting. Human product/content review remains essential prior to treating materials as authoritative policies.
- Why: help a studio use handoff procedures with its current stack and understand PhotoTrackly's intended coordination focus; not proof of paid product adoption, image-editing capabilities or ROI.
- Accuracy: keep initial human manual assignments, human QC, unconfirmed integrations and pre-launch status explicit. Do not infer other providers' features from these examples.
- Dates: retain historically verified `datePublished` on original articles; mark `dateModified` only for pages updated with the new practical assets on 12 October 2026.

Do not broaden a content inventory simply because prompts can generate additional pages. Prefer focused updates with original evidence and sensible internal navigation; preserve the four existing commercial category owner URLs.
